# FoundUps Earth vision

## Outcome

FoundUps Earth is the shared spatial manifestation of the FoundUps ecosystem. It shows what is happening at a place, how confidently it is known, which FoundUps are acting there, what work remains, and whether conditions improved.

It turns place-based evidence into coordinated, verifiable action without treating people as trackable objects.

## Core loop

1. A person, device, public feed, or FoundUp produces an observation.
2. AutoPost may capture video, audio, images, position, orientation, time, transcript, and device evidence.
3. GeoSync normalizes the submission into a provider-neutral `GeoEvent`.
4. The 3V engine evaluates verification, validation, and valuation.
5. FoundUps Earth manifests the event with its provenance and confidence state.
6. RedDog links the condition to an existing FoundUp or proposes a bounded FoundUp launch.
7. Participants perform work and submit receipts.
8. New observations verify whether the intervention changed the condition.

## Beyond incident reporting

AutoPost is a general interaction surface, not merely a reporting camera. A prompt such as “RedDog, I want to play” may switch AutoPost into an experience finder, field game, discovery walk, AR mission, civic quest, or other spatial mode.

The platform therefore models observations, experiences, opportunities, assets, work, and outcomes. It does not prematurely prescribe tapping, scanning, voice, AR overlays, or another interaction method. Those are replaceable clients over shared contracts.

## First applications

- GotJunk: dumping, reusable goods, cleanup requests, collection routes, and verified removal.
- FoundUps House: physical hubs, active FoundUps, tools, events, energy, food, and local resources.
- Environmental recovery: runoff, pollution, erosion, dirty beaches, damaged habitats, and remediation.
- Disaster response: flooding, fire damage, landslides, damaged roads, supply gaps, and recovery work.
- Spatial play: place-aware games and missions that can discover or contribute useful local knowledge.

## Constitutional line

FoundUps Earth maps conditions, systems, resources, and consented participation. Named-person search, covert individual tracking, face recognition, doxxing, and inference of protected personal locations are outside its purpose.
